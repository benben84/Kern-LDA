# RightForm LDA — Yelp slideshow

Animated five-frame deck for RightForm LDA (Bakersfield / Kern County), sized for a Yelp Photo Slideshow.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Slides advance on their own. Arrow keys, on-screen buttons, and swipe move between frames.

## Yelp upload files

`frames/slide-1.png` through `frames/slide-5.png` are 1200×1200 PNG (under 2MB). Yelp crops slideshow photos to 4:3 from the center, so the type sits inside that band.

Suggested captions, in order:

1. RightForm LDA — court-ready documents in Bakersfield. Not a law firm.
2. You choose the forms. We prepare, file, and serve them at your direction.
3. Divorce, guardianship, eviction, small claims, wills, powers of attorney, and LLCs.
4. Competitive rates, with discounts for seniors, veterans, and disabled clients.
5. Intake, prepare, file. Book a consultation at rightformlda.base44.app.

Copy stays inside what RightForm publishes: a non-attorney legal document assistant for self-represented clients. The slides do not offer legal advice or court representation.

Add `?crop=1` to the page URL to shade the top and bottom strips Yelp will crop. Add `?export=1&slide=0` (slide 0–4) to hold one finished frame for a screenshot.
